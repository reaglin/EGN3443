EGN3443 – Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 2 / Module 1 — Statistics for Engineers

This module introduces foundational statistical concepts relevant to engineering practice, including descriptive statistics, measures of central tendency and spread, data variability, and the role of statistics in evidence-based decision-making.
