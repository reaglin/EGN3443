Week 2/Module 1 - Statistics for Engineers

This module introduces engineers to the foundational statistical concepts essential for data-driven decision-making in technical environments. Learners will explore key topics such as descriptive statistics, probability distributions, and data variability, examining how these principles apply to real-world engineering problems. By the end of the module, participants will be equipped to interpret statistical data accurately and apply analytical techniques to support engineering analysis and quality control.
