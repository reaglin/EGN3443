Week 12/Module 11 - Regression Analysis

In this module, learners explore the foundations of regression analysis, examining how to model and quantify relationships between variables to support data-driven decision-making. Through practical application, learners develop skills in building, interpreting, and evaluating both simple and multiple regression models. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to apply regression techniques to real-world datasets and draw meaningful conclusions from their results.
