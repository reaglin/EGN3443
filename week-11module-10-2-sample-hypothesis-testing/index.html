Week 11/Module 10 - 2 Sample Hypothesis Testing

This module introduces learners to two-sample hypothesis testing, exploring how to compare means or proportions across two independent or paired groups. Learners will examine the underlying assumptions, select appropriate test statistics, and interpret results within real-world contexts. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to confidently design and execute two-sample tests to support data-driven decision making.
