Week 9/Module 8 - Hypothesis Testing

In this module, learners explore the fundamental principles of hypothesis testing, including how to formulate null and alternative hypotheses, select appropriate test statistics, and interpret p-values and significance levels. Through practical examples and guided exercises, learners develop the ability to apply common hypothesis tests to real-world data and draw statistically sound conclusions. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to critically evaluate claims, identify potential errors in testing, and communicate findings with confidence and precision.
