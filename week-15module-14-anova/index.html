Week 15/Module 14 - ANOVA

In this module, learners explore Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) as a statistical method for comparing means across three or more groups. Through theoretical grounding and practical application, participants examine the assumptions underlying ANOVA, interpret F-statistics, and distinguish between one-way and multi-factor designs. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to determine when ANOVA is the appropriate test, conduct the analysis, and draw meaningful conclusions from their results.
