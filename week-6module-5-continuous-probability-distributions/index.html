Week 6/Module 5 - Continuous Probability Distributions.

In this module, learners explore the key characteristics and applications of continuous probability distributions, with a focus on the normal, exponential, and uniform distributions. Through worked examples and practical exercises, participants develop the ability to calculate probabilities, interpret distribution parameters, and apply these concepts to real-world scenarios. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to select and use appropriate continuous distributions to model and analyse data in professional and research contexts.
