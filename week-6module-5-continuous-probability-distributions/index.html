EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 6 / Module 5
Continuous Probability Distributions

This module extends your probability toolkit from discrete to continuous random variables. You will explore probability density functions (PDFs), cumulative distribution functions (CDFs), and the key continuous distributions used in engineering practice — including the uniform, normal, exponential, Weibull, and log-normal distributions — through readings, worked examples, visualizations, and graded assessments.
