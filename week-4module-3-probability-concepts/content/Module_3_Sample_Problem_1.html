Module 3 Problem 1. Mutually Exclusive Events: Circuit Failure Analysis

This problem explores a scenario where an electronic circuit can fail due to one of three components (capacitor, resistor, or transistor), with each failure mode being mutually exclusive.

Key concept: When events are mutually exclusive, the probability of any one of them occurring is the sum of their individual probabilities: P(A or B or C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C)

Solution: The total probability of circuit failure is 0.03 + 0.02 + 0.04 = 0.09 or 9%.

Python Solution (link)

# ======================================================================
# 1. Mutually Exclusive Events: Circuit Failure Analysis
# ======================================================================
print("Problem 1: Mutually Exclusive Events - Circuit Failure Analysis")
print("---------------------------------------------------------------")

"""
Problem:
An electronic circuit can fail due to one of three components: the capacitor (C),
the resistor (R), or the transistor (T). Based on historical data, the probabilities
of failure are:
- P(C) = 0.03 (capacitor failure)
- P(R) = 0.02 (resistor failure)
- P(T) = 0.04 (transistor failure)

Assuming these failures are mutually exclusive (only one component fails at a time),
what is the probability that the circuit fails?
"""

# Python solution
P_C = 0.03  # Probability of capacitor failure
P_R = 0.02  # Probability of resistor failure
P_T = 0.04  # Probability of transistor failure

# For mutually exclusive events, P(A or B or C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C)
P_failure = P_C + P_R + P_T

print(f"Probability of capacitor failure: {P_C}")
print(f"Probability of resistor failure: {P_R}")
print(f"Probability of transistor failure: {P_T}")
print(f"Total probability of circuit failure: {P_failure}")
print(f"Probability that the circuit works: {1 - P_failure}")
print()
