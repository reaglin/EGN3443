Module 3 Sample Problem 2

2. Independent Events: Manufacturing Quality Control

In this problem, we analyze quality control checks for gears where each check is independent of the others.

Key concept: For independent events, the probability of all events occurring is the product of their individual probabilities: P(A and B and C) = P(A) × P(B) × P(C)

Solution: The probability that a gear passes all three checks is 0.95 × 0.98 × 0.97 = 0.9033 or 90.33%.

Python Solution (Link)

# ======================================================================
# 2. Independent Events: Manufacturing Quality Control
# ======================================================================
print("Problem 2: Independent Events - Manufacturing Quality Control")
print("-----------------------------------------------------------")

"""
Problem:
A manufacturing process produces gears for industrial equipment. Each gear
goes through three independent quality checks:
- Check 1: Dimensional accuracy (passes with probability 0.95)
- Check 2: Material hardness (passes with probability 0.98)
- Check 3: Surface finish (passes with probability 0.97)

What is the probability that a randomly selected gear passes all quality checks?
What's the probability at least one check fails?
"""

# Python solution
P_check1 = 0.95  # Probability of passing dimension check
P_check2 = 0.98  # Probability of passing hardness check
P_check3 = 0.97  # Probability of passing surface finish check

# For independent events, P(A and B and C) = P(A) × P(B) × P(C)
P_all_pass = P_check1 * P_check2 * P_check3

# Probability that at least one check fails = 1 - P(all pass)
P_at_least_one_fail = 1 - P_all_pass

print(f"Probability of passing dimensional check: {P_check1}")
print(f"Probability of passing hardness check: {P_check2}")
print(f"Probability of passing surface finish check: {P_check3}")
print(f"Probability that a gear passes all checks: {P_all_pass:.4f} or {P_all_pass*100:.2f}%")
print(f"Probability that at least one check fails: {P_at_least_one_fail:.4f} or {P_at_least_one_fail*100:.2f}%")
print()
