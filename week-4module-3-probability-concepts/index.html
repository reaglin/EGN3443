Week 4/Module 3 Probability Concepts

This module introduces foundational probability concepts, guiding learners through key principles such as sample spaces, events, and the rules governing likelihood. Learners will explore both theoretical and experimental probability, developing the ability to calculate and interpret probabilities in a variety of real-world contexts. By the end of the module, participants will be equipped to apply core probability rules confidently and accurately in data analysis and decision-making scenarios.
