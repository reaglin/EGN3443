EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 4 / Module 3: Probability Concepts

This module introduces foundational probability theory essential for engineering analysis. Topics include sample spaces, probability axioms, theoretical versus experimental probability, core probability rules, and Monte Carlo simulation methods.
