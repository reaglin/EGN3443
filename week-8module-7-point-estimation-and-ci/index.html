EGN3443 · Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 8 / Module 7 — Point Estimation and Confidence Intervals

This module develops the foundational tools of statistical inference: deriving point estimators (sample mean, sample proportion, standard deviation), understanding sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem, and constructing confidence intervals for population means. Use the artifacts below to study the theory, work through interactive examples, and complete the graded assessments.
