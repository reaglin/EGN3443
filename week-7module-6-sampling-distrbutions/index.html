EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 7 / Module 6 — Sampling Distributions

This module explores the foundations of statistical inference, focusing on sampling distributions, population parameters versus sample statistics, the Central Limit Theorem, standard error, and confidence intervals. Use the resources below to read, watch, practice, and assess your understanding.
