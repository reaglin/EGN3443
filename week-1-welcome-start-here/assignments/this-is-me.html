This is Me!

📣 Week 1 Discussion: Self-Introduction

In order to build a supportive and inclusive classroom environment and foster a sense of community, I invite each student to introduce yourself by answering the following questions:

  1. What is your full name, where are you from, and what are you currently studying or majoring in?
  2. What is your working experience and educational background?
    Do you have any prior experience or background knowledge related to this course? If so, please share a little about it.
  3. What are your academic and career goals?
    How do you hope this course will help you achieve them?
  4. What motivated you to enroll in this course?
    Is there a particular topic or skill you are excited to learn about?
  5. What are some of your interests and hobbies outside of academics?
    Do you have any favorite books, movies, or activities?
  6. Share one fun fact about yourself that others might find interesting or surprising.

This discussion serves as an opportunity for students to:

  • Build community
  • Foster connection
  • Reduce feelings of isolation

By sharing your backgrounds, interests, and goals, you begin to see one another as real people, rather than just names on a screen. This activity also helps me, as your instructor, to better understand your diverse experiences and learning needs—and adjust my teaching accordingly.

✅ Submission Instructions and Tips:

  • Feel free to include a photo or video if you’re comfortable!
  • Start your post by answering the above 6 questions and you will receive 5 points.
  • Respond to at least one classmate’s introduction to start the conversation and you will receive another 5 points.
