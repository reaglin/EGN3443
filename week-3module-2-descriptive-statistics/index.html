EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 3 / Module 2 — Descriptive Statistics

This module introduces the foundations of descriptive statistics, covering measures of central tendency (mean, median, and mode) and measures of variability (range and variance) within engineering contexts.
