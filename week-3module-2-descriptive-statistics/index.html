Week 3/Module 2 - Descriptive Statistics

In this module, learners explore the core concepts of descriptive statistics, including measures of central tendency, variability, and data distribution. Through practical examples and exercises, learners develop the ability to summarise and interpret datasets meaningfully. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to select and apply appropriate statistical measures to communicate key insights from data clearly and accurately.
