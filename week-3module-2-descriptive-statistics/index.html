EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 3 / Module 2 — Descriptive Statistics

This module introduces the fundamental tools used to summarise and interpret data. You will explore measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode), measures of variability (range, variance, standard deviation), and data distributions — building the statistical vocabulary every engineer needs for rigorous data analysis.

Topic Details
Introduction to Descriptive Statistics
Topic Detail .html

This reading introduces descriptive statistics as the practice of summarising, organising, and presenting data, giving real-world examples of its use and establishing the conceptual foundation for the measures covered later in the module.

Measures of Central Tendency
Topic Detail .html

This reading explains the three main measures of central tendency — mean, median, and mode — describing what each represents, how each is calculated, and when each is the most appropriate summary of a typical value in a dataset.

Measures of Variability
Topic Detail .html

This reading covers measures of variability — including range, variance, and standard deviation — explaining how each quantifies the spread of data around a central value and why spread matters even when two datasets share the same mean.

Data Distribution
Topic Detail .html

This reading describes how data values form distributional patterns, covering concepts such as frequency distributions, histogram shapes, skewness, and symmetry so learners can recognise and interpret the overall structure of a dataset.

Selecting and Applying Appropriate Statistical Measures
Topic Detail .html

This reading guides learners and instructors on how to choose the correct descriptive statistics for a given dataset by matching measures of central tendency and variability to the appropriate level of measurement and data type to avoid misleading conclusions.
