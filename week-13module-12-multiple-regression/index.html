EGN3443 — Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 13 / Module 12 — Multiple Regression

This module extends the ideas of simple linear regression to models with two or more predictor variables. Explore the learning resources, reference materials, quizzes, and assignments below to build and evaluate multiple regression models in engineering contexts.
