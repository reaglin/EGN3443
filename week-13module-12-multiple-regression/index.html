Week 13/Module 12 - Multiple Regression

In this module, learners explore multiple regression analysis as a powerful extension of simple linear regression, examining how two or more predictor variables can be used to model and explain variation in a continuous outcome. Learners will investigate key concepts including model specification, interpretation of regression coefficients, and the assessment of model fit using statistical measures such as R-squared and adjusted R-squared. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to construct, evaluate, and interpret multiple regression models using real-world data, strengthening their ability to draw meaningful conclusions from complex datasets.
