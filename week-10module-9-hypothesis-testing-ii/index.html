Week 10/Module 9 - Hypothesis Testing II

Building on foundational concepts in hypothesis testing, this module guides learners through more advanced inferential techniques, including two-sample tests, paired comparisons, and an introduction to non-parametric methods. Learners will develop the skills to select appropriate tests based on data type and research context, interpret results with confidence, and understand the conditions under which each method is valid. By the end of the module, learners will be equipped to apply these techniques to real-world datasets and draw statistically sound conclusions.
