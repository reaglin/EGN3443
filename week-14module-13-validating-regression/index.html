EGN3443 – Probability & Statistics for Engineers

Week 14 / Module 13 — Validating Regression

This module focuses on the critical step of validating regression models after they are built. Learners will examine model assumptions (linearity, homoscedasticity, and independence), perform residual analysis, identify influential observations, and apply cross-validation techniques to assess model generalizability. Use the resources below to read, practice, and demonstrate mastery.
